# Scene 1 — giant coffee bean

**Deliverable:** one vertical 9:16 clip, 9 seconds, 1080p, with generated audio,
no captions, no music, no voice.

## Mechanism preserved from the reference

`impact → crack → hands open it → camera enters → interior connects`

| Beat | What must be visible |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0–0.5s | one mallet strike on a **sealed** central seam |
| 0.5–1.5s | a thin irregular crack zips downward, **after** contact |
| 1.5–3.0s | mallet dropped; both hands grip the crack lips and peel the rind outward, edges curling under her fingers |
| 3.0–5.0s | she ducks through; camera travels forward through **the same** opening; coffee skin sweeps past both lens edges |
| 5.0–7.0s | interior: curved continuous walls of the same bean, caramel fibers, cream built-in seating, bean-shaped table, one steaming cup |
| 7.0–9.0s | she sits on the cream seat; camera settles |

## Must not happen

- the two halves sliding apart like rigid doors
- wall slabs falling away
- a dissolve or morph at the threshold instead of physical camera travel
- a generic rectangular wooden room inside (straight planks)
- the opening appearing without her hands causing it

## Concept change from the source

Source was a giant fruit house. Ours is a **roasted coffee bean** — interior
material vocabulary follows the exterior: brown shell, caramel fiber, cream
upholstery, coffee-colored light.

## Character lock (same in every asset of this scene)

Adult woman, brown bob, sage work jacket, cream trousers, brown boots, one
wooden mallet. Garden, soft golden morning light.

## Known correction, do not skip

The first generated still came out with a **window and a chimney**, which reveals
the surprise before the strike. `prompts/image-correction.txt` removes them.
Approve the corrected still before animating.

## Reference strategy for this scene

**One** starting image, **one** video generation, **no separate interior image.**
Separate interior references encouraged a visible change of space at the
threshold. The trade-off accepted here: the shot never establishes the whole
bean in a wide view.
